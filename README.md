# Malevolent Meat Master

A pig with a flamethrower roasts mutants for meat. This page only hosts the Windows test builds.

## Download

**[Download v0.6.9 for Windows](https://github.com/LichIndex/MMM-Malevolent-Meat-Master/releases/download/v0.6.9/MalevolentMeatMaster_v0.6.9_windows.zip)** (43 MB). Older versions are on the [Releases page](https://github.com/LichIndex/MMM-Malevolent-Meat-Master/releases).

1. Unzip it anywhere (the Desktop is fine).
2. Double-click `MalevolentMeatMaster_v0.6.9.exe`. Nothing else to install.
3. Windows may say *Windows protected your PC*, because the game isn't code-signed. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. It only asks the first time.

It needs 64-bit Windows 10 or 11 and a graphics card with OpenGL 3.3 (almost any from the last ten years). If it opens to a black window or closes at once, update the graphics driver. If it runs slowly, set **Settings > FIRE EFFECTS** to LIVE LOW or CLASSIC.

## How to play

You're a pig with a flamethrower, roasting mutants for meat. Pick a destination on the **Map** tab and press **FIGHT**. Aim with the mouse and hold the left button to burn. Fire stacks burn damage on whatever it touches, so sweep the horde and keep them all burning. Swap fuel tanks with Q, the mouse wheel or 1–4. Watch your heat: let it max out and the gun starts hurting you, so ease off the trigger to cool down. Dead mutants drop raw meat, materials, sauce ingredients and sometimes gear. The longer you stay, the higher the threat climbs, and the tougher the mutants and the better the loot. When you've had enough, press **E** to call the food truck and survive until it arrives. If you extract, you keep everything. If you die, you lose the run's meat and gear plus part of your materials.

Back at the Smokehouse you turn the meat into money. In the Kitchen, brew a sauce from your ingredients and put it on your raw meat; raw meat spoils over time and sauced meat doesn't. The smoker on the food truck then smokes it, fast during a fight and at half speed between runs, even with the game closed. The truck's counter sells the cooked meat in real time, even while you're out fighting, to whichever faction's turf it's parked on. A sauce that matches their taste pays more. That money (Energy Sprinkles), plus your materials, buys new fuel stills, better gun parts at the Workbench and upgrades around the yard. You equip it all on the **Loadout** tab, push to higher threats, and work your way up to top-tier gear and the Infested endgame.

| | |
|---|---|
| Aim | Mouse |
| Burn | Hold left mouse (or Space) |
| Swap tanks | Q, mouse wheel, 1–4 |
| Call the truck (extract) | E |
| Pause / menu | Esc |
| Flamethrower sheet | Tab |
| Fullscreen | F11 or Alt+Enter |

## Your save

Saves are in `%APPDATA%\MalevolentMeatMaster_v069\` (paste that into the Explorer address bar). Each version keeps its own save: when you get a newer version, press **IMPORT v0.6.9 SAVE** on its main menu before you start to bring your progress along.

This is a test build: the numbers, art and sounds are placeholders and will change.
